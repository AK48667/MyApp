What Is the Gospel?

November 2026

The Gospel is the good news that God loves the world and sent His Son, Jesus Christ, to save sinners.

Jesus died on the cross for our sins, was buried, and rose again on the third day. Through faith in Him, we receive forgiveness, eternal life, and reconciliation with God.

“For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in Him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” - John 3:16

Core Truths of the Christian Faith

  • God is holy and righteous.
  • All people have sinned and need salvation.
  • Jesus Christ is the Son of God.
  • Jesus died for our sins and rose again.
  • Salvation is a gift of grace through faith in Christ.
  • Those who trust in Christ receive eternal life.
