# God's Word

A simple website dedicated to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

## About

This site exists to share the message of salvation found in Jesus Christ and to encourage people through Scripture.

> "For God so loved the world, that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life." - John 3:16

## Topics

- The Gospel
- Salvation by Faith
- Bible Studies
- Christian Living
- Prayer and Encouragement

## Local Testing

Open:

index.html

in your browser.

## Deployment

Push changes to GitHub.

The website is automatically published through GitHub Pages.

## Mission

To proclaim Jesus Christ, make disciples, and share God's Word with the world.
